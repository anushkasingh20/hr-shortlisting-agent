Data Analyst – FinTech Platform

About the Role:
We are looking for a sharp Data Analyst to join our growing analytics team at a fast-scaling FinTech company. You will turn raw financial data into actionable insights that drive product and business decisions.

Minimum Requirements:
- 0-2 years of experience in data analysis or related field
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Experience with data visualization tools (Power BI or Tableau)
- Hands-on experience with Excel and Google Sheets
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Statistics, or related field

Preferred / Nice-to-Have:
- Knowledge of Machine Learning basics
- Experience with Pandas and NumPy
- Familiarity with dashboarding and KPI reporting
- FinTech or banking domain exposure

Key Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets to identify trends and business insights
- Build interactive dashboards and reports for stakeholders
- Write complex SQL queries for data extraction and transformation
- Collaborate with product, engineering, and finance teams
- Present findings clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences

Domain: FinTech
Seniority: Junior
Location: Mumbai / Remote (India)
